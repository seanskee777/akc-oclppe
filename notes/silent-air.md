# Silent Air

A place where artists share transparently, and artists are the advertisement.

## The problem it answers

An artist puts their own music up for free. They go to listen to their own
playlist, their own station, and it is interrupted by adverts in the middle of
a mix. The music is theirs. The interruption is not, and there is no setting
anywhere that says *not over this*.

The only current fix is to sell the music, and selling brings the same
advertising back — with a cheque instead. So the choice on offer is between
being interrupted and being paid to be interrupted.

## The shape of it

Not a recommendation graph with money on it. A **referral chain that pays
forward.**

1. Someone arrives, referred by an artist, and that artist gets **$50**.
2. The next person referred automatically receives that same $50.
3. They are expected to pass it on, and to fund the next referral, as they go.
4. A referred artist therefore gets discovered *and* funded by another artist.

The advertisement is the art. The artist is the person listening. The artwork is
whatever happens emotionally — which is not measurable and was never meant to
be.

## Where AKC-OCLPPE touches it

Not to work out who gets paid. To answer *"did I get paid?"* and *"were they
soft?"* — a visible record of where a track travelled and who ended up hearing
it. The money is unresolved. Being legible is not.
